# My SWOT Analysis

*October 17, 2024*

## Strengths:

Let’s kick things off with my **strengths**! (giving the guy with an ego an opportunity to write about his strengths—good idea, guys! 😂) I think my biggest one is my **creativity**. I love coming up with new ideas and thinking **outside the box**. I’ve also got a solid grip on **math and logical thinking** and can usually tackle tricky problems without too much hassle. Plus, I’m pretty **curious**; if something piques my interest, I’m **stuck to the chair for hours!**

## Weaknesses:

**Weaknesses... WOO.** The most subjective topic. I could describe them with two words: **writing blogs** (lol). Now seriously. I’ll admit I can get a bit **distracted** sometimes. I might start working on something and then get sidetracked by random things, and **BOOM**—I'm on my phone watching some dude dressed as Cookie Monster chase pigeons. It takes me a little while to get back on track, but once I get going, I'm **unstoppable**.

## Opportunities:

Looking ahead, there are some cool **opportunities** coming up. I’m really looking forward to the **international week** and **project week**—I can finally put my skills to the test (and **flex what I can**)! Plus, I can’t wait for my first **internship** in my third year. I really want to try working in an **actual work environment**! I also spend time on my **hobby projects**, which are a great way to learn and keep things interesting.

## Threats:

And then we have the **threats**. Honestly, I don't think I can think of any, but if I had to, it would probably be myself. Sometimes I get a little too excited and rush through things, which makes me miss important details. I need to **slow down** and really focus.

## End:

In the end, my goal is to become a **great developer**—someone who creates stuff that people actually enjoy using. I want to make things that are not just useful but also **pretty cool**. I’m ready for whatever comes next!

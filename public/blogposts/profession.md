# Who I Would Work For?

*October 17, 2024*

I am really getting tired of writing blog posts, but this is the last one.

So who would I like to work for? Well, ideally, I would love to work for **myself**. Being my own boss and having success in that. I have plenty of ideas that need realization anyways, so why not? But before I get to the point where I have enough experience to do that, I’d love to try working for a big company like **Google** or **Meta**.  
*Kind of basic*, I know, but I just want to see how it is in such a big company with brilliant minds as my co-workers. I hear the salary is great too, looool. Gotta get that money 🤑.

Jokes aside, I think these companies provide the perfect environment for development as a software engineer. I would be *extremely* happy if I had the opportunity to experience that.
